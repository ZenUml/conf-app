# Contact and action policy

## Match the offer to responsibility

- **Verified site administrator or site-wide procurement/licensing owner:** lead with Full only. Explain site-wide use, unified licensing and the upgrade. Do not include the $299 single-space alternative, even if no reseller is listed.
- **Actual user or space owner:** a verified single-space need can justify the single-space option. Confirm scope and current pricing. Do not assume every user owns a space or can approve spending.
- **Unverified role / technical contact:** state the known relationship, use Full as the site-wide context when relevant, and gently ask them to forward to the colleague responsible. Do not call them admin or invent decision authority.
- **Reseller/partner:** procurement channel is separate from contact responsibility. Label public Full list pricing as such; let the existing partner confirm their final quote. Do not imply a quote was approved or that we contacted the partner when neither happened.

Use the shared live Marketplace pricing implementation for Full. The locally configured single-space price is an internal reference, not proof of a current checkout price. Do not hardcode seat break-even points or compute annual price as ten monthly payments.

## Message shape

1. Remind them which app and host product this concerns. After a gap, link the verified Marketplace listing using its actual name, including Confluence where relevant.
2. Give one or two useful verified facts: relevant space count, actual active accounts, a concrete unmet need or prior request. State the window and distinguish views, accounts, saves and inventory. Do not infer blocked engineers' intentions from event counts.
3. Express interest in helping. "One of our most active teams" needs a comparable ranking; otherwise simply say the team is using it actively. Do not fabricate superlatives.
4. Ask one easy next step: feedback, forwarding to the right colleague, or whether a quote would help. Avoid interrogating them about budget, ownership and procurement all at once.

The user's priority is adoption and converting a good-fit customer; flexibility can be discussed without inventing a discount or committing to an unapproved commercial term.

## Authorisation follows the actual request

Track action, actual recipient(s) or explicit batch, channel, content/template scope, timing and any constraints. An approved administrator is not permission to contact all administrators. A one-off batch is fixed to its authorised members. A genuinely dynamic or recurring authorisation follows the user's stated scope and end condition; do not add an arbitrary expiry.

Queries, analysis, drafts and tracker organisation do not confer sending or license-grant authority. Explicit permission already present in the conversation remains effective; do not ask again merely because a skill is entered or an item changes. Clarify only missing information needed to execute or a material action outside the scope. Where necessary, present the finished draft before asking.

"Don't send" overrides an earlier send plan. "I sent it" means inspect Sent and record the actual message, not send the draft again. Scheduled messages require their own verified status; cancellation must be explicit before marking cancelled.

These Markdown policies do not remove tool access from the agent; they are workflow rules, not technical isolation.
