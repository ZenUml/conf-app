## Facts that change how to read these numbers

- **The T1 prior window is 14 days, not 30.** The rollout tag was published 08-23 01:24Z, and all 283 prior-window clickers clicked after that.
- **The template gallery comparison is confounded.** `auto_first_open` fires only on an empty new macro. Journeys with no gallery include returning authors and macros that were already filled. The prior window also has 336 successes outside any journey (67 in the current window), so prior success rates are less reliable.
- **Volume is concentrated.** The largest tenant holds 54% of homepage_feed_viewed, 35% of viewer_source_opened, 41% of Lite copy_for_ai_impression, and 30% of Lite macro_create_succeeded events in the current window.
- **"First-ever" means first seen since 2026-04-18** (start of Mixpanel tracking), not lifetime first.

## OPEN QUESTIONS
- Production state of the Forge flag create-guide-enabled. The Create guide shows zero external events, which could mean the flag is off or that exposure is too recent.
- Why does has_edit_permission read true on 100% of viewer_source_opened events? Either canUserEdit always resolves true, or the button appears only for editors. This blocks any reader-vs-editor split of View Source.
- T1 lands in the 30-99 band, for which the 2026-08-22 decision log defines no action. Someone has to decide whether this means continue, iterate, or stop.
- T4 (page-banner activation variant) depended on the combined T1 + T8-A readout, and T8-A never shipped. Does T4 still have a decision trigger?
- macro_create_started with entry_point=byline fires only 15 times against 308 byline_diagram_created events. Byline-attributed creates cannot be read without a time join, so it is unknown whether this is a tracking gap.
- Is the Copy for AI drop below the 2% rule on the current window (1.77%) a trend or noise? The rule was meant to be a one-time verdict.
